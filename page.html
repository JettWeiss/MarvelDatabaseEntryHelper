Synopsis for "Christmas Time Warp!"

Franklin uses the Time Platform as a last ditch effort to get his family Christmas presents. After going through time and collecting many gifts he ends up losing them all to a dinosaur and returning to the present breaking the time platform in the process. His family however is just glad that he's there presents or no.

Synopsis for "Now You See Me..."

Franklin attempts to use his father's Inviso-Ray to see through his birthday presents but ends up making himself and things around him invisible instead. Franklin the drops the now invisible Inviso-Ray and looses it. H.E.R.B.I.E. helps him out by pouring powdered sugar on Franklin and around the room in order to make everything visible. Once they located the Inviso-Ray Franklin uses it to fix everything and H.E.R.B.I.E. cleans up the powdered sugar mess just in time before Sue walks in. Franklin accidently reveals his plan of peeking his presents but his mom acts confused as says what presents showing him the empty closet. Franklin leaves the room confused and forgetting his mom has the power to make things invisible as well.
